A single deadpan one-line forecast for "Deadpan Forecasts for Cities That Do Not Exist": "Tonight in Wobbleford, fog thick enough to lose a bus in — visibility down to one lamppost and a rumor." The fog is drawn as one concrete, startling image — thick enough to swallow a bus — and then the visibility report quietly swaps metres for "one lamppost and a rumor", a measurement no real meteorologist would ever read out, delivered as if it were completely normal. The entry deliberately avoids the crowded angles other forecasts lean on, and lets the sting land on the final word instead.
